Dara Varam

PhD in EDEE @ École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, specifically at the Embedded Systems Laboratory (ESL). Previously I did my MS in Machine Learning and my undergrad in Computer Engineering and Mathematics @ AUS with a minor in Data Science. I'm Iranian and I'm 24 years old.

From May 2025 to October 2026, I was a visiting researcher at the Senseable City Lab @ MIT.

My research focuses mainly on model compression, optimization and quantization for neural networks and LLMs. I have also done studies in training optimization, computer vision, and eXplainable AI.
